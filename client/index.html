Why Tempfile?

Tempfile is built with a simple motive: to provide a clean, secure, and minimal way to share files temporarily. We share so many documents, images, and videos every day that only need to be accessed for a short time. Keeping them indefinitely wastes storage and increases privacy risks.

By automatically expiring uploads after a specified duration—from a single day up to one week—Tempfile ensures your digital footprint remains small. There are no accounts to create and no tracking involved. It's just fast, ephemeral file sharing designed for the modern web.
